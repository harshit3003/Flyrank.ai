# Portfolio Proof Statement

## One-Paragraph Proof Statement

I build **usable AI-powered web interfaces** that turn complex AI capabilities into clear, interactive experiences for users. This portfolio is for **a hiring manager or engineering lead at an AI-focused startup looking for a frontend/AI engineer**, and the one thing I want them to do after seeing it is **contact me for an interview**. The projects here are meant to prove that I can take an AI capability, design a practical frontend around it, connect the interface to real functionality, and turn it into a working product rather than just a visual mockup.

## Why this needs to exist

My CV and LinkedIn can show my skills, projects, and certifications, but they cannot fully prove that I can actually turn AI and frontend knowledge into usable products; this portfolio gives a hiring manager the evidence to see that capability in action.
