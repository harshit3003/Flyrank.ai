01

The Problem

Agricultural trade is historically burdened by heavy intermediation. Connecting farmers (sellers) directly with wholesale buyers (traders) presents significant technical and logistical challenges.

The core problems this platform solves are the difficulty of direct agricultural trade, including transparent price discovery, live negotiation and communication between buyers and sellers, managing active crop listings, understanding market prices (mandi rates), and handling the mathematical constraints of transport logistics.

02

What I Did

I engineered a complete two-sided marketplace architecture designed specifically for agricultural trading. The system separates the workflows of farmers and traders into distinct, role-based dashboards while uniting them through a real-time negotiation engine.

Frontend Architecture

The client is a single-page application built with React and bundled via Vite. I utilized TailwindCSS for rapid, responsive styling. To handle the complex geographical nature of agriculture, I integrated Mapbox-GL and Leaflet for mapping functionalities. For data visualization (e.g., market trends), I integrated Chart.js. Framer Motion was used selectively to ensure smooth UI transitions.

Backend & Persistence

The server operates on Node.js and Express.js, exposing secure REST APIs. The persistence layer is hybrid: utilizing MongoDB (via Mongoose) for flexible document storage of crop listings and user profiles, alongside SQLite. Authentication is strictly enforced using bcrypt for password hashing and JWT (JSON Web Tokens) to securely isolate the seller (farmer) and buyer (trader) endpoints.

Real-Time Negotiation & Bidding

To solve the negotiation problem, I implemented a full duplex WebSockets layer using Socket.io. This system allows buyers to submit live bids on crop listings. Sellers receive these bids instantly and can execute a state-machine workflow to Accept, Reject, or issue a Counter-offer. The real-time chat system runs parallel to the bidding engine to facilitate direct communication.

Buyer Demand & Market Intelligence

Instead of relying purely on seller supply, the platform features a reverse-procurement mechanism (buyer demand requests) where buyers can explicitly communicate the crops they need, allowing sellers to fulfill them. I also integrated a market intelligence module that displays relevant mandi price data, and built a logistics/profitability calculator to help users understand transport costs prior to finalizing a bid.

03

What Came of It

I successfully built a working, highly-interactive full-stack agritech marketplace that brings multiple, complex parts of agricultural trading into a single application.

The final implementation proves the viability of combining standard marketplace listings, distinct role-based workflows, live WebSockets-based negotiation, order management, and market intelligence tools into one cohesive digital product.
