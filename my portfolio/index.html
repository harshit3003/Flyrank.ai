02

What I Did

While building this portfolio and my other projects, I used AI as a tool that I direct and check, rather than something that builds everything independently. My process:

  • Choosing the design and layout of my portfolio.
  • Selecting the projects and technologies (C++, Python, Full-Stack) to showcase.
  • Using AI to generate or improve code.
  • Running the AI-generated code locally to verify functionality.
  • Checking the output manually and identifying errors.
  • Using AI to help fix identified issues and adjusting the design to meet requirements.
03

What Came of It

Because the internship is still ongoing, final project outcomes are not yet complete.

However, the main outcome so far is that I have learned to use AI practically. Instead of simply accepting its output, I evaluate the code, test it locally, and guide the AI to correct mistakes.
