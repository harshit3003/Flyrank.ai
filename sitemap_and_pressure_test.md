# Portfolio Sitemap & Pressure Test

## 1. The Foundation

**Claim / Proof Statement:**
"I am an AI/ML-focused software developer who builds practical, user-focused applications using modern web technologies and AI."

**One Action:**
Contact me / Get in touch.

---

## 2. Sitemap Sketch

**Home**
├── **Hero:** Intro, CTA
├── **Projects:** Case studies (KishanMarket, AI/ML Project)
├── **About:** Intro + skills
└── **Contact:** Email, links

---

## 3. Pressure-Test Output (AI Strategist Feedback)

**Key Feedback Received:**

1. **Weak Claim Formulation:** 
   Words like "practical" and "user-focused" are filler adjectives that sound generic. The claim describes the developer rather than the specific outcome created for the client/recruiter.

2. **Missing Proof of AI/ML in Structure:** 
   The AI/ML focus isn't immediately obvious. A recruiter scanning the page won't see the AI/ML expertise until they dig into a specific project card.

3. **CTA Friction:** 
   Relying only on a Contact section at the bottom kills momentum. Users reading the About section or finishing a Case Study need an immediate next step.

4. **Project Ordering:** 
   Projects should be ordered by strength, not chronology. 

**Actionable Changes I Will Make:**
- **Refine the Hero:** Lead with a specific one-line value prop. Add two CTAs (Primary: "View My Work", Secondary: "Contact Me"). Make sure the subtitle explicitly names the AI/ML angle.
- **Distribute CTAs:** End each case study with a repeated CTA ("Like what you see? Let's talk") and add a soft CTA to the end of the About section.
- **Reorder Projects:** Put the strongest work (like KishanMarket and the AI/ML project) up front.
